## What Threestrand Is

Threestrand is a digital wellness app designed to help you reduce phone addiction by:
- Blocking distracting apps
- Providing accountability through partners and families
- Tracking your app usage
- Supporting your goals with self-imposed time limits

Threestrand is a tool to support your digital wellness journey. It is not a medical device, therapy, or treatment for addiction.

## Intended Use & Abuse Prevention

### Threestrand Is For Voluntary Accountability

Threestrand is designed for **voluntary, consensual accountability** between adults who choose to support each other, and for **families**, where a parent or guardian and their teen use Threestrand together, openly.

### Prohibited Uses

**This app must NOT be used to:**
- Monitor or control someone without their genuine, freely-given consent
- Coerce, pressure, or force someone into limiting their phone use
- Facilitate domestic abuse, stalking, or controlling behavior
- Monitor anyone who has not freely chosen to participate
- Track a partner's location or communications
- Install or run Threestrand on a teen's phone without the teen knowing it is there, or hide from them what their parent can see
- Add a child under 13 to Threestrand

### Your Right to Leave

**Any adult may disconnect from a partner or leave a family at any time**, for any reason, without the other person's permission. This is by design. We cannot and will not prevent it.

A teen in a family leaves through their parent or guardian, because the family is how the parent takes part. A teen can still always uninstall Threestrand, and nobody can prevent that; the family is told the app went quiet.

### If You Are Being Coerced

If you are being forced to use this app against your will, or if someone is using it to control you:

- **You can uninstall the app at any time** - no one can prevent this
- **National Domestic Violence Hotline**: 1-800-799-7233
- **Text "START" to 88788** for help via text message
- **TheHotline.org** for online chat support
- **Teens**: Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline, **1-800-422-4453** (call or text), or **988** for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

We take abuse seriously. If we become aware that Threestrand is being used for coercive control, we reserve the right to terminate the abuser's account.

## Accountability Partnerships

### How It Works
You can connect with one or more accountability partners. When connected:
- Your partner sees whether your protection is set up and holding, and the requests you send them (see the Privacy Policy for exactly what is shared)
- You ask your partner when you want temporary access to a blocked app, and they answer
- Both people receive notifications about important events

### Mutual Consent
By entering a partner's code and completing the connection, you confirm that:
- You are freely choosing to participate
- You understand what data you will share (see Privacy Policy)
- You understand what data you will receive about your partner
- No one is forcing or coercing you into this partnership

### Disconnecting
Either adult partner may disconnect at any time by going to Settings and removing the partnership. Disconnecting:
- Takes effect immediately
- Stops all data sharing
- Does not require the other partner's approval
- Allows both users to continue using Threestrand independently

We do not notify the other partner of the reason for disconnection.

## Families

A family on Threestrand is a household: one or two adults, and optionally their teens.

- **Adults** in a family are partners to each other and approvers for the teens. Either adult can answer a teen's request.
- **Teens** ask a parent when they want temporary access to a blocked app. A teen cannot loosen their own setup or leave the family on their own; they can always make their setup stricter.
- **Family time (Phones away)** pauses apps on every phone in the family for a set time. Calls, emergency services and Threestrand itself always stay available.
- **An adult who admits a teen** confirms they are that teen's parent or legal guardian, and that the teen knows Threestrand is on their phone and what the family can see.
- **When the last adult leaves** a family, the family ends and any teens in it are released from it.

## Safety Features

Certain features cannot be disabled to ensure your safety:

### Always Accessible
The following are always accessible, even when blocking or family time is on:
- **Emergency services** (911, emergency dialer)
- **Your phone app** and incoming calls
- **System settings**
- **Threestrand itself**
- On Android, also the **Play Store** (for updates) and **your keyboard apps**

### Boot Grace Period
If enabled, blocking is paused for 60 seconds after your phone restarts. This ensures you can always access Settings if something goes wrong.
