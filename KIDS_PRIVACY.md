# Anchor Kids Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 31, 2026 (Version 1.0)

Anchor Digital LLC ("we," "us") makes **Anchor Kids**, a service that lets a parent or
guardian set up and manage a child's phone. It has two parts: the **Anchor Kids app** that
runs on the child's phone, and the **Anchor Kids parent app** the parent uses to manage it.
This policy covers both.

Anchor Kids is intended to be set up by a **parent or legal guardian** for their own child.
The parent configures the child's phone, chooses who the child can contact, and decides what
information the service collects. We designed Anchor Kids as a partnership, not surveillance:
the child can always see that the app is installed, and it can be removed from device Settings.

> This policy covers Anchor Kids only. The separate Anchor accountability app for adults has
> its own policy at [anchorph.one/privacy](https://anchorph.one/privacy).

---

## Summary

- We collect only what's needed to run the service the parent set up
- Text messages and call logs stay on the child's phone and never reach our servers
- Location is on demand and latest only. We do not keep a location history
- We don't sell your data, show ads, or use children's information for profiling
- A parent can delete a child's data at any time from the parent app

---

## Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to operate the service the parent set up. From the **child's
phone**, the app may send the following to our backend (Google Firebase) so the parent can
see it in their app:

- **Approximate and precise location**, only **when the parent requests it on demand** from
  their app, or as configured by the parent. We store only the most recent location, not a
  location history.
- **Device status**: battery level, charging state, cellular signal level, network
  connectivity, and whether the phone is still in its locked-down state. Used to show the
  parent the phone is online and set up correctly.
- **Safety and tamper signals**: whether the child left the Anchor app, switched the home
  launcher, opened the device Settings, or opened other apps (including the package names of
  those apps), and whether app-blocking is on. Used to alert the parent if the phone's
  protections are removed.
- **Unlock and PIN events**: when a parent PIN is entered on the child's phone, and the time
  and success of attempts, so the parent can approve unlocks and see attempted access.
- **SOS and emergency events**: when the child uses the SOS button.
- **Pairing information**: the codes and identifiers used to link a phone to a family.

**Contacts** the child can call or message are **created by the parent** in the parent app.
The child's phone does not upload the child's existing contacts.

**Text messages and call logs stay on the child's device.** The Anchor Kids app reads SMS and
call information **on the device** to display the child's conversations and recent calls and
to match them to parent-approved contacts. Message contents and call-log details are **not
transmitted to our servers** and are not visible to us.

From the **parent's phone**, we collect the parent's account email and basic profile, and (if
enabled) a notification token so we can send alerts.

## How we use information

- To operate the features the parent enabled: calling, messaging, contacts, location, SOS.
- To alert the parent to safety and tamper events, and to let them approve unlocks.
- To keep the service working: authentication, and syncing settings between the apps.
- We do **not** sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with third
  parties for their own purposes.

## How information is shared

- **Within the family.** Information from the child's phone is shown to the parents and
  guardians in that child's family who have been given access in the parent app.
- **Service providers.** We use **Google Firebase** (authentication, database, hosting,
  messaging, crash reporting) to operate the service. Firebase processes data on our behalf
  under Google's terms. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Legal.** We may disclose information if required by law.

## Children's privacy (COPPA and GDPR-K)

Anchor Kids is set up by a parent or guardian for their own child. The parent provides
consent, configures the service, and controls the data collected. We collect from a child
only the information described above, and only to provide the parental service the parent
requested. A parent can review the child's information, change what is collected (for example
by turning features off), and delete it at any time by removing the child's profile in the
parent app, which deletes the associated data. To request deletion or ask questions, contact
us below.

We do not knowingly use children's information for advertising or profiling, and we do not
require a child to disclose more information than is reasonably necessary.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as the family uses the service. Removing a child's profile in
the parent app deletes that child's data (status, location, contacts, logs, alerts) from our
backend. Removing the Anchor Kids app from the child's phone stops further collection. To
request full account deletion, see
[Account Deletion](https://github.com/hrweaver/anchor-legal/blob/main/ACCOUNT_DELETION.md) or
contact us at the address below.

## Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Backend data is stored in Google Firebase with
access restricted to the family and to us as the operator.

## Your choices

- Turn features such as location and messaging on or off in the parent app.
- Remove a contact, a child profile, or the whole account.
- Uninstall the Anchor Kids app from the child's phone at any time.

## Changes

We'll update this policy as the service changes and revise the "last updated" date.
