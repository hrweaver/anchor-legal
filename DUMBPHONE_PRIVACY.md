# Anchor Dumb Phone Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 7, 2026

Anchor Dumb Phone has no account, no analytics, and no server.

## What the app stores, on your phone only

- The tools you chose and the apps that fill them.
- The wait you chose, and any wait in progress.
- Whether setup is complete and whether the one-time purchase is unlocked.
- The moment you agreed to the Terms and Conditions, the Terms version, and the app version.
- Whether you ticked the two setup boxes for the emergency alert reminder and the digital assistant.

All of it lives in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## What leaves the phone

Nothing, except the one-time purchase, which Google Play processes under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app never contacts a server of its own.

## Special access the app asks for

- **Accessibility service.** Used only to notice when an app comes to the front and send you home if it is not one of your tools. It does not read screen content for any other purpose, records nothing, and sends nothing anywhere.
- **Notification access.** Used only to clear notifications from apps that are not your tools. Notification content is not read, stored, or sent.
- **Do Not Disturb access.** Used only to keep the phone silent for apps that are not your tools, while letting calls, messages, alarms and calendar events through.

## Crash reports

The app includes no crash-reporting library. Google Play may show the developer anonymised crash and stability statistics collected by Android itself, under Google's terms.

## Children

Anchor Dumb Phone is for adults 18 and older. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
