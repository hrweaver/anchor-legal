## What Anchor Dumb Phone Is

Anchor Dumb Phone is an Android home screen. You choose a small set of tools to keep on the home screen. Every other app stays installed but is sent back to the home screen when it opens, and its notifications are cleared. Turning the app off, or adding a tool later, means sitting out a wait you chose, with the phone in front of you.

It is not a medical device, a safety device, a parental control, therapy, or treatment for anything.

## What Always Works

- Phone calls, including calls to emergency numbers, work whether or not the Phone app is one of your tools.
- Incoming calls, alarms, and government emergency alerts are never silenced by the app.
- Android Safe Mode turns the app off on any phone.

## What May Not Work

Any app that is not one of your tools, and anything that depends on one, may be unavailable until you have sat out the wait you chose. That can include apps you might want in a hurry: banking, rideshare, messaging apps other than SMS, two-factor authentication codes, health and medical apps. Think about those before you choose your tools and your wait. If you rely on a specific app for your safety or health, keep it as a tool, or do not use Anchor Dumb Phone.

Links in text messages will not open, because the app never offers a web browser as a tool.
